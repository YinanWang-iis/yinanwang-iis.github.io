Research interests

Explore my research

X-ray absorption spectroscopy (XAS), electron energy-loss spectroscopy (EELS), and atomistic simulation.

Computational Physics

Ab initio calculations like DFT, BSE-GW & AIMD. Especially in  improving theoretical calculations of core-loss spectra

Data-Driven Analysis 

Spectroscopic analysis methodology based on machine learning and artificial intelligence. 

Measurement Informatics 

Developing and applying informatics to accelerate scientific discovery by systematically integrating and analyzing complex experimental data.

Machine Learning Force Field (MLFF)

 Development of generalizable MLFF, utilizing on-the-fly training strategies to achieve efficient reproduction of ab initio calculations. 

2D materials

 Manipulating electronic states and dielectric response in 2D materials through atomic-scale doping and First-Principles modeling
